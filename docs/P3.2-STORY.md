## The ending (as written)

**ELENA:** If you're reading this, congratulations. You're officially the first person to finish my game.

Unless you're me. In which case, stop cheating.

One day, I'll make a real one.

## How Elena is shown

Elena never appears in person and is never described in a profile. She is heard in what she left behind: signs that argue with the player (“If it doesn’t work, the bug is your keyboard”), a TODO list that ends with “sleep (optional)”, a cat she insists is a lifestyle, a secret garden she cut because “nobody will ever find it”, and a library book overdue since 1997. She is funny, stubborn, a little rude to her own game, and she keeps backups. Mara learns who she was by playing her game, and the last line is the one thing Elena never said out loud.
