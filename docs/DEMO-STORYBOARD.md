Target: under three minutes, recorded from real gameplay. Every shot must come from the browser suite's screenshots or a screen recording of a real session. Do not use mock-ups or edited states.

|---|---|---|---|

| 2:45–3:00 | Show the repository: `docs/game-design/`, `.kiro/`, the `npm test` output, and the CI run link | Terminal and browser capture | Specs, tests and replay evidence |

## Capture checklist
- Record at 1280×900 for the main shots and 390×844 for the mobile check.
- Keep the replay evidence (`npm test`) on screen for at least five seconds.
- Do not publish the video or any screenshot until the owner authorises it.
- The video must be uploaded only to a channel the owner controls, and must not exceed three minutes.
