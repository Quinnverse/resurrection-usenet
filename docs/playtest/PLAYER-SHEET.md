# Before you play · 开始之前

**English**

Thank you for playing. This is a story game, about 30–45 minutes long. There is no right or wrong way to play and
you cannot lose. We are testing the game, not you.

- Move with **W A S D**, look with the **mouse**. **E** or a left click to use things and to continue dialogue.
- **H** gives a hint if you are unsure. **Esc** pauses (settings, language, subtitles, sound).
- Other keys are explained in the game when you need them.
- Please play as you would at home. Think aloud if you like; it helps us.
- The game asks two quick questions after each chapter. Answer with your first impression, or skip.
- You can stop at any time.

**中文**

感谢参与。这是一款剧情游戏，大约 30–45 分钟。没有对错的玩法，也不会失败。我们测试的是游戏，不是你。

- **W A S D** 移动，**鼠标** 转动视角。**E** 或鼠标左键用来互动和继续对话。
- 不知道怎么做时按 **H** 获取提示。**Esc** 暂停（设置、语言、字幕、声音）。
- 其他按键会在需要时由游戏说明。
- 请像在家里一样玩。愿意的话可以边玩边说出想法，这对我们很有帮助。
- 每章结束时游戏会问两个简短问题，凭第一感觉回答或跳过即可。
- 你可以随时停止。

(Gamepad and touch screen are experimental. 手柄和触屏为实验性支持。)
