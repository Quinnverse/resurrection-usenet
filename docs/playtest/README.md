# V5 blind playtest — facilitator guide · 主持人指南

**Status: no session has been run.** Everything in this folder is the kit; `RESULTS.md` stays empty until real
players have played. Do not fill it with guesses.

状态：尚未进行任何测试。本文件夹只是测试工具包，`RESULTS.md` 在真人试玩之前保持为空，不得填入推测内容。

## What is in the kit

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `resurrection-v5-playtest.zip` (built by `npm run v5:rc` or `node tools/v5-playtest-pack.mjs`; also the CI artifact `resurrection-v5-rc1-package`) | **The folder you give a player.** `START-HERE.html` (what to do, controls, time, what is recorded), `Resurrection-V5-Playtest.html` (the whole game with the recorder, one file, opens by double-click: no Node, no server, no internet), `feedback-form.html` |
| `PLAYER-SHEET.md` | the same instructions as `START-HERE.html`, for reading aloud or printing |
| `FEEDBACK-FORM.md` / `feedback-form.html` | questions after the session; the HTML version saves a JSON file; every question is optional |
| `SESSION-LOG-TEMPLATE.md` | your own notes for each session (facilitator), including the owner's own first play |
| `RESULTS.md` | generated by `node tools/v5-playtest-summary.mjs docs/playtest/sessions/*-game.json` |
| `sessions/` | put each session's two JSON files here, named by tester id |
| `../V5-AUDIO-CHECKLIST.md`, `/v5/audio` | the separate listening check (not part of the player folder) |

**Consent and privacy.** The game records nothing until the player presses Start or Continue, and the title screen says
what is recorded (times per chapter with clock start and end, pauses, skips, hints, stalls, page exits, choices, the 1–5
answers, frame rate, and browser, screen, core count and graphics-card name). No name, email, location, camera, microphone
or screen content. Nothing is sent anywhere: the record stays in the player’s browser until the player downloads it and
chooses to hand it over. The pause menu has *Delete my playtest record*. Tester ids are your own (`P01`…); do not ask for
names. Do not collect recordings of players without their explicit agreement.

**Order.** The owner plays once alone first (`SESSION-LOG-TEMPLATE.md`, section “Owner”), fixes anything that blocks a
stranger, and only then invites 5–8 others.

## Who · 招募

- 5–8 people who have not seen the design documents, the script or any build. Not developers of this project.
- At least 2 who mainly play in Chinese and 2 in English; at least 2 who rarely play 3D games; ideally 1 with a gamepad and 1 on a touch screen (both are experimental).
- Get consent for screen and voice recording. Players are anonymous: use ids like `P01`.

## Before each session · 每场之前

1. Unzip `resurrection-v5-playtest.zip` somewhere new for each player (a fresh folder; or use a new browser profile or a private window) so there is no earlier save or record. Players can also do this on their own machine: send them the zip and ask them to read `START-HERE.html`.
2. Open `Resurrection-V5-Playtest.html`. On the title screen check that the notice about what is recorded is visible. Type the tester id into the field (`P01`…).
3. Press F3 once to see the frame rate line (bottom left, with the graphics card name), then F3 again to hide it.
4. Hand over the keyboard and mouse (or pad). Start screen and audio recording only if the player agreed.
5. Phone or tablet players: run `npm run v5:lan` on your computer and give them `http://<your address>:8090/v5/?playtest=1`. The record then lives in the phone’s browser; ask them to download it at the end.

## During · 进行中

- Say only what `PLAYER-SHEET.md` says. Do not explain the story, the controls or the archive.
- Help only after **3 minutes** without progress, and only with the smallest hint ("try pressing H"). Write down the time, the place and what you said.
- The game asks two 1–5 questions after each chapter. The player answers or skips; do not prompt.
- Note anything the player says aloud (laughing, "what?", "oh no", silence after a scene), with the time.
- If they want to stop, let them. A partial session is still data; mark it unfinished.

## After · 结束后

1. At the end (or in the pause menu, Esc → "Download playtest log") download the game log JSON.
2. The player fills in `feedback-form.html` (about 10 minutes, every question optional) and downloads its JSON. Paper is fine too.
3. Put both files in `sessions/` as `P01-game.json` and `P01-form.json`. Keep recordings outside the repository.
4. After all sessions: `node tools/v5-playtest-summary.mjs docs/playtest/sessions/*-game.json > docs/playtest/RESULTS.md`.

## What the game log records · 游戏记录的内容

Real time per beat and per chapter, with the pause menu, title and rating screens excluded, and the clock start and end of each chapter; pauses (count and total time); page exits and tab switches; frame-time histogram per beat (average, slow share, worst, approximate 1% low); every hint shown after
idling and every press of H; idle stretches over 30 s with position and the nearest prompt (likely stuck spots);
retries, falls and refused Tab switches; skipped dialogue; the choices made; frame rate, slow-frame share and worst
frame per beat; JS memory when the browser reports it; which input devices were used; browser, screen, cores, memory
and the GPU name. Nothing is sent over the network; it stays in the browser until downloaded.

## Release thresholds (from `../V5-PLAYTEST-PLAN.md`)

- median first play 35–45 min, reached without padding;
- no chapter with a median of more than 2 stalls;
- at least 70% can say in their own words that June lost her credit and her job, and that the ending restores the credit without changing the 1998 and 2004 versions;
- nobody describes June as only Elena's love interest.
