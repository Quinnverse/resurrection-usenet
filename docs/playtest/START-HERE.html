Resurrection — The Summer We Never Finished

A story game in a browser, in English or 中文. Thank you for helping us test it. You are testing the game, not yourself: there is no right way to play and you cannot lose.

一款在浏览器里玩的剧情游戏，支持英文和中文。谢谢你帮我们试玩。我们测试的是游戏，不是你：没有“正确”的玩法，也不会失败。

1 · Start · 启动

Double-click the file Resurrection-V5-Playtest.html in this folder. It opens in your browser. Nothing to install, no internet needed. Use a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer. Turn the sound on and, if you can, wear headphones.

双击本文件夹里的 Resurrection-V5-Playtest.html，它会在浏览器中打开。无需安装，也不需要联网。请用较新的 Chrome、Edge、Firefox 或 Safari。请打开声音，最好戴耳机。

Open the game · 打开游戏

If you see “WebGL unavailable” or a black screen: update the browser, or try another browser. Tell the person who gave you this folder.

如果看到“WebGL 不可用”或黑屏：请更新浏览器或换一个浏览器，并告诉把文件夹交给你的人。

2 · How long · 大约多久

About 30–45 minutes for the whole story. This is an estimate: nobody has timed a full play yet, and your time is one of the things we want to learn. You can stop at any time, and you can pause with Esc.

整个故事大约 30–45 分钟。这只是估计：还没有人完整计时过，你的用时正是我们想了解的。你可以随时停止，按 Esc 暂停。

3 · Controls · 操作

Keyboard + mouse · 键鼠W A S D move · mouse look · E or left click: use / continue the dialogue (hold E for things like restoring) · Space jump · Shift run · Tab switch 1998 ⇄ 2004 in the town · 1 2 3 pick an answer · H hint · Esc pause
Gamepad · 手柄 (experimental · 实验性)Left stick move · right stick look · A jump / continue / pick · X use (hold) · B skip a line you have seen · Y switch era · Back hint · Start pause · D-pad moves through answers and menus
Touch · 触屏 (experimental · 实验性)Left half of the screen moves, right half looks around; use the round buttons for jump, E, ⇄ (era), ? (hint) and ⏸ (pause)

Keys can be changed in the pause menu → Settings. · 按键可在暂停菜单 → 设置中修改。

4 · What the game records, and what happens to it · 记录什么、怎么处理

This copy of the game keeps a play record in your browser. When you press Start or Continue, it begins to note: how long each chapter takes (with start and end times), when you pause, skip a line, use a hint or seem to be stuck, the choices you make, your 1–5 answers after each chapter, how smoothly the game runs, and basic details of your device: browser, screen size, number of processor cores and the name of the graphics card.

It never records your name, email, location, camera, microphone, what you type outside the game, or anything on your screen. It sends nothing anywhere: the record stays inside this browser until you choose to download it.

You decide. At the end (or in the pause menu) press Download playtest log and give the file to the person who gave you this folder, or don’t. Press Delete my playtest record in the pause menu at any time to erase it. You can also leave without giving anything back. The questions after each chapter can be skipped.

这份游戏会在你的浏览器里保存一份游玩记录。点击“开始”或“继续”后，它会记录：每章用时（含开始和结束时间）、暂停、跳过、使用提示或疑似卡住的情况、你做的选择、每章结束后你的 1–5 分评价、游戏运行是否流畅，以及基本设备信息：浏览器、屏幕尺寸、处理器核数和显卡名称。

它不会记录你的姓名、邮箱、位置、摄像头、麦克风、你在游戏之外输入的内容，也不会记录你的屏幕。它不会向任何地方发送数据：记录只留在本浏览器里，直到你自己决定下载。

由你决定。结束时（或在暂停菜单里）点击“下载试玩记录”，把文件交给给你这个文件夹的人——也可以不交。随时可以在暂停菜单点击“删除我的试玩记录”清除它。你也可以什么都不交就离开。每章后的问题都可以跳过。

5 · If you get stuck · 卡住了怎么办

Press H for a hint. If you are still stuck after a few minutes, that is useful to know: tell the person who gave you this folder where you were. · 按 H 获得提示。如果几分钟后仍然卡住，请告诉我们你卡在哪里，这对我们很有用。

6 · Afterwards · 玩完之后

  1. Download the playtest log (pause menu, or at the end). · 下载试玩记录（暂停菜单或结尾处）。
  2. Open feedback-form.html, answer what you like in your own words (every question is optional), and press Save. It downloads a file. · 打开 feedback-form.html，用你自己的话回答想回答的问题（都是选填），点击“保存”下载文件。
  3. Give both files to the person who gave you this folder. · 把两个文件交给给你这个文件夹的人。

Thank you. · 谢谢你。
