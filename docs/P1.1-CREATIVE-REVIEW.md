## 3. Remaining subjective concerns (not changed)

- Foreshadowing is thin before Act III. Echo's blanket is set up only by one journal entry and one record.
- Corruptor's first two lines sound alike ("Show them the edit" and "Then hold it"). They differ in tone but not in rhythm.
- The Last Letter slots are fixed sentences chosen by state. Two players with the same branch will read identical letters apart from the closing.
- The "Revision N" counter in the workspace header is developer-facing. It is kept because the reload test uses it.
- The Echo ending's warm palette is a colour change, not a change in prose; the ending does not tell the reader how its memory has altered.

## 4. Simulated blind-play review

**Simulated review, not human playtesting.** It was produced by reading the UI text and the screenshots as a player with no prior knowledge.

- Opening: clear. The transmission and its contradiction are visible without instructions.
- After "Open transmission": the next steps are unlabelled actions ("Inspect header: …") with no explanation of why they matter. A player may not connect them to the contradiction.
- Decisions: "Provisionally reconstruct" is unclear. A player may not know what reconstruction changes until after the preview.
- Act 2 action order: "Trace provenance" is the highlighted primary action, while the other options look equally important. The order of the investigation is not obvious.
- Propagation panel: the caption and hop list are accurate but dense. A player may skip them.
- Last Letter composer: sealing is clearly marked irreversible and requires confirmation.
- Endings: each ending explains its result. The memory-divergence list makes the cost of each choice visible.

Misleading or confusing steps found: "Reconstruct" and "Provisionally" are two different ideas in the same label; "Voices · trust" numbers have no explanation.
