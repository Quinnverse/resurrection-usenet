# V5 blind playtest plan (not yet carried out)

Nobody outside development has played V5. The 35–45 minute target, whether the game is fun, whether the story lands
and whether the controls are clear are all **unverified** until this is done.

## Who

- 5–8 players who have not read the design documents or the script, and have not seen the game.
- At least 2 who mainly play in Chinese and 2 in English. At least 2 who do not play 3D games often.

## Setup

- Desktop Chrome, Firefox or Safari, keyboard and mouse, headphones. One session with a gamepad if possible.
- `npm start`, open `http://127.0.0.1:3000/v5/` in a fresh browser profile (no save). Language set by the player.
- Screen and voice recording with consent. The observer does not help except when the player is stuck for 3 minutes.

## What to record

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Time per chapter and total | The chapter marker appears at the top left; note wall-clock time at each. |
| Stuck moments | Any time the player says they do not know what to do, or 60 s without progress. Note where and whether a hint (idle or H) helped. |
| Control problems | Camera, jump/glide, Tab, the train camera, the bike, the beam, the pen, tuning. |
| Reading | Did they read dialogue or skip it? Subtitle size complaints. |
| Story | After each chapter: one sentence on what just happened. After the end: who June is, what Elena did, why the letters stay unsent. |
| Feelings | 1–5: fun, moving, confusing, too long, too short, per chapter. |
| Audio | Music and sound effects: pleasant, annoying, too loud or quiet. |
| Bugs | Anything visibly wrong, with the time on the recording. |

## Pass marks for release

- Median first play 35–45 minutes, without padding.
- No chapter with a median of more than 2 stuck moments.
- At least 70% can say in their own words that June lost her credit and her job, and that the ending restores the
  credit without changing the 1998 and 2004 versions.
- No player calls June "only Elena's love interest" when asked who she is.
