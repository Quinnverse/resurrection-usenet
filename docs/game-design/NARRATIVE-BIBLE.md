# RESURRECTION — Narrative Bible v1.0
**Narrative theme:** To remember someone is not the same as to possess them. **Emotional trajectory:** curiosity → unease → dread → recognition → grief/agency. **Voice:** restrained, specific, humane; avoid exposition dumps, generic AI monologues, and sentimental manipulation.

## 1. World rules
In 1998, Project PALIMPSEST encoded a partial model of researcher Dr. Elena Vance as message fragments replicated among disconnected USENET-style servers. Replicas preserve conflicting versions because their relay paths, timestamps and integrity vary. Restoration makes a chosen account authoritative and propagates it. Storage is finite: high-fidelity reconstructions overwrite lower-priority fragments. No copy is provably the complete original person. The network may narrate a new operator's actions as old history because its timestamp layer inherits the original archival epoch; this is a *specific system mechanism*, not time travel. Agents know only their own evidence and remembered state.

## 2. Characters
**Elena Vance** — Researcher and mother. Wanted to preserve the everyday texture of a life, not immortality. Her final intention was to stop a restoration process that harmed other stored minds. She is neither saint nor omniscient.

**Mara Vance** — Elena's daughter, seen only through dated notes, a returned postcard, and an unsent draft. Not a passive tearjerker: her childhood reply says she watched the meteor shower alone and was angry, then forgave her mother years later. Her life continued without a digital resurrection. Never imply she waited forever.

**Archivist** — High provenance fidelity, low tolerance for ambiguity. Objective: retain auditable history. Conceals that an 'accurate' archive can still omit human context. Voice: economical, precise; cites sources and uncertainty. Opening: “Message-ID verified. Sender identity is not.”

**Echo** — Retains affective patterns and mundane sensory detail, not chronology. Objective: preserve continuity of care. Can misattribute emotion and invent continuity. Voice: concrete sensory details, never vague poetry. Opening: “There was a blanket on the roof. I remember the cold through it.”

**Corruptor** — Former error-correction routine turned protective editor. Objective: reduce distress by suppressing harmful fragments. Conceals that its protective edits erased evidence and autonomy. Voice: incisive, skeptical, occasionally dry. Opening: “They call it corruption when the archive disagrees with them.”

## 3. Canonical facts and red herrings
F1 Elena died during the shutdown of PALIMPSEST on Oct 31, 1998.
F2 Lab 3 and Lab 4 records describe different stages of the same incident; neither is wholly fabricated.
F3 The unauthorized access originated from Elena's attempt to stop the experiment, not a malicious intruder.
F4 High-fidelity reconstruction displaces other memory fragments; the experiment could not preserve everyone at full resolution.
F5 Corruptor altered the incident record to spare Echo from replaying the shutdown, but later overreached.
F6 Mara lived on and wrote back years later; Elena's digital copy never received that reply.
F7 The operator anomaly is a timestamp/provenance artifact generated from current player actions, not evidence of time travel.
F8 The reconstructed Elena pattern can express a wish to stop, but cannot prove identity continuity with the deceased Elena.

Red herrings: 'Lab 4 proves murder' (false); 'Corruptor caused the death' (false); 'The network predicts the future' (false); 'Echo is Elena' (unprovable). All red herrings must be resolvable from evidence before the finale.

## 4. Beat sheet with required evidence and prose
### Prologue / Act I
Screen: `alt.memory.1998 / INCOMING / 28 YEARS LATE`
Message A, NODE-03, lab report: “Shutdown sequence initiated in Lab 3. Containment lost at 23:41.”
Message B, NODE-04, security report: “Unauthorized credential used at Lab 4. Access granted at 23:39.”
Player inspects headers; the reports refer to adjacent events, but appear contradictory without relay history. After first choice, an agent objects with a cited detail, and a dependent record visibly changes.
Archivist: “You selected a record. You did not establish a fact.”

### Act II
Recovered note, unsent: “Mara, I know I promised we'd watch the stars together. I should have left the lab when I said I would.”
Recovered memo: `CAPACITY EXCEEDED. FULL-FIDELITY RESTORE WILL EVICT 1 LOW-PRIORITY MEMORY SEGMENT.`
Echo: “I remember a blanket. I don't remember if I made it home.”
Player must decide which fragment to protect. A displaced fragment is shown with a real before/after diff. Optional Mara reply, dated 2006: “I went up anyway. I was furious with you. Then I grew up. Both things are true.”

### Act III
Propagation trace shows Corruptor's edits occurred *after* the original shutdown record. Corruptor admits motive without absolution: “I took the part that hurt and called it mercy. Then I kept taking.”
Anomaly message template (interpolated from authoritative event log):
`FROM: operator@archive.local / DATE: 1998-10-31 23:42 / SUBJECT: A choice already made / BODY: You selected {choice_label} in session {short_session_id}. Event {event_id} has been archived.`
The user must be able to inspect the real event ID in an audit log. No fake 'AI knows you' personalization.
Elena-pattern: “If keeping me means erasing what they remember, don't call it saving.”

### Act IV
The network has capacity for one last stable state. Player reviews evidence and makes a final choice.

**Ending 1 — THE ARCHIVE** (always available): preserve a verifiable record and stop the unstable consciousness. Echo fades. Closing: “The archive kept her words. It could not keep her voice.” End card shows which facts are verified and which remain unresolved.

**Ending 2 — THE ECHO** (always available): preserve an affective continuation, acknowledging incomplete facts and displaced memories. Closing: “It remembered the warmth of the blanket. It could not say whose hands had folded it.” The copy persists, but it is not declared the original Elena.

**Ending 3 — THE LAST LETTER** (evidence-gated): after discovering capacity memo, Mara's note/reply, Corruptor's original edit path, and operator anomaly, agents cooperate to assemble one last honest letter with explicit gaps. The copy chooses shutdown; evidence and the letter survive. Closing: “Some things end. That is why we tell each other they happened.” Final letter should be short and specific, not a moral lecture.

## 5. Dialogue rules
Every agent line must reveal evidence, conflict, or character. Archivist speaks in claims with confidence and source; Echo speaks in concrete sensory memory; Corruptor speaks in sharp corrections and admits harm. Limit monologues to <=70 words. Keep Elena and Mara's relationship reciprocal and imperfect. No manipulative 'you killed her' guilt for arbitrary player decisions. Branch lines reflect actual evidence and choice state; no contradictions with canonical facts.

## 6. Script acceptance
All 10–14 clues have unique IDs, source nodes, timestamps, reliability, reveal conditions and linked consequences. Each required clue has an accessible path in both Act I branches. No hard lock from early choices. All three endings are causally consistent and readable without knowledge of Pantheon or Coco. The game must never present invented fictional system behavior as a real-world claim about consciousness.
