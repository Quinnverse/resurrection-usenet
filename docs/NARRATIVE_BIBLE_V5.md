# RESURRECTION V5 — Narrative Bible
Status: CANONICAL CREATIVE TARGET; scene details are proposed until implemented.

## Logline
After her mother Elena dies, 26-year-old Mara reopens *Last Light*, a coastal game Elena co-created in 1998 with June. In the reconstructed 1998 summer, the player embodies 17-year-old Elena and shares a creative, mutually romantic-adjacent bond with June. In the present, Mara restores the work's provenance, without rewriting the loss, harm, or June's autonomy.

## Characters
- **Elena / skylark**: 17 in 1998, 23 in 2004. Reserved programmer, composer, witty and defensive, talented but capable of selfish decisions. Later altered a commercial build, removed June's attribution and changed June's characters. Her remorse cannot cancel accountability.
- **June / junebug**: 17 in 1998, 23 in 2004. Artist, writer, co-author, outgoing and ambitious. Has her own career, choices and legitimate material losses; not Elena's muse, villain or prize. Their attraction is reciprocal, never formally labeled.
- **Mara**: 26 in the present; daughter and restoration operator. Restores authorship, not a parent's idealized biography.
- **Theo**: scripted lighthouse keeper, lovable, comic, longs for the lamp to work.
- **Iris**: character created by June, has her own trajectory and the option to leave town. Elena's 2004 modification removed this agency.

## Chronology
1998: shared summer, train trip, photography, lighthouse design with crooked window, original guitar melody and near-confession; departure for June's own opportunities. The 1998 game is incomplete.
2004: Elena releases modified commercial version, removes June's credit and some authored events; June has lost recognition and work opportunities. In a cliffside cottage they confront one another and part without reconciliation.
Later: Elena drafts multiple unsent letters, increasingly accountable; never presumes June's forgiveness. Present: Mara reconstructs archived materials, restores verified credit and enables a newly authored seasons extension. Elena is deceased; June's later life is her own.

## Anchors and playable beats
**Train, 1998**: June and Elena each secretly photograph the other. June says '你就是风景' in a playful, earned exchange. Photograph backs: June '她以为我在拍风景。' / Elena '我知道。' The photos are kept in the player's session album, not searched for as evidence.
**Waiting motif**: June waits for Elena to catch the train ('快点，我等你'); later June departs ('你可以想我，但不要把自己留在这里'); ending requires moving forward rather than endlessly waiting. This is an original motif, not a quotation or representation of any living persons' private relationship.
**Lighthouse**: June draws an asymmetrical window. Elena complains about the work but builds it. June '你可以不听我的呀。' Elena '可是我想听你的。' The same window persists in later versions.
**Music**: Elena plays an ORIGINAL melody. June asks '再弹一次'; '感我此言良久立' is from Bai Juyi's public-domain poem 《琵琶行》; credit source in production notes. No copyrighted existing-song playback.
**2004 cottage**: Elena '你还记得那天吗？' June '我记得。我也记得你把我的名字删掉了。' June '我问的是，我的损失怎么办？' Elena '我真的很想你。' June '我知道。可我的损失怎么办？' Their pain is dual; Elena's wrongdoing remains concrete. June later says only '路上小心' as Elena leaves.
**Unsent apology**: Elena acknowledges affection, harm, owed credit and apology; '灯塔不是为了让船停在原地。是为了让她能去想去的地方。' This is not delivered to June by Mara.
**Final**: Restore June's credit ('Elena — Programming & Music; June — Art & Story'), preserve historical versions and separately create a seasons extension. Theo: '这里怎么会下雪？' Mara: '因为有人终于允许夏天结束了。' The snow is NEW, not a rediscovered 1998 feature.

## Character ethics and limits
No fake perfect reconciliation, forgiveness on June's behalf, dead-person chatbot, supernatural proof, trauma-as-puzzle, romantic excuse for economic harm, or reduction of June's future to waiting. Do not depict real Xu Fei/Li Na fan interpretations as established facts. Song '姐姐我等你' and other existing copyrighted music are only thematic references; write original score and dialogue.

## Narrative presentation
Use short lines, gesture, silence, blocking, eye contact and environment instead of exposition. In the first ten minutes players should enjoy June's presence before learning the later dispute. A historical memory scene is a dramatized reconstruction with uncertainty disclosed outside the cinematic flow, not a literal recording.
