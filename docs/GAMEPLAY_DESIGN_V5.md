# RESURRECTION V5 — Gameplay Design
Status: TARGET. Replaces evidence-first loop of GAME_DESIGN_BIBLE V1.

## Core loop (2–4 min)
Character invites or world visibly needs repair -> player performs active movement/interaction -> archive/scene/NPC responds -> permanent visible and emotional payoff -> seamless next beat. No open-ended clue collection or compulsory archive UI.

## Main controls (remappable)
WASD movement, mouse camera, Space jump/context glide, Shift sprint/cycle boost, E context interaction/cooperate, Tab time shift (only when narrative and collision safe), left click photo/action, Esc pause. Gamepad equivalents, subtitles, reduced motion, input assists, skip/replay dialogue, instant retry. Time shift cannot strand player in geometry: precompute safe landing or postpone shift with subtle feedback.

## Abilities
**Restore**: apply archive-backed object manifests to scene graph and physics (bridge, window, lamp); requires a real parsed record, not animation only.
**Revive**: restore archive-backed NPC behavior/dialogue and schedule (Iris bakery and departure).
**Rewind**: switch 1998/2004 build-state data; change terrain, animation, actors, lighting, collision and audio, not just color.
**Recompose**: create clearly NEW contemporary edition content (seasons, animation extensions); never attribute this to historical authors as recovered work.

## Movement and chapter toys
- Train chase: sprint, vault simple obstacle, catch June's hand. No punitive fail; June waits and offers a retry.
- Camera: hold aim and click photo; June reacts with shy/playful animation and short branching lines. No mandatory angle-match or score. Store player photo choice for ending callback.
- Bicycle: fixed scenic coastal route with acceleration, steering, optional overtaking; June adjusts speed to player, no racing loss.
- Lighthouse: position crooked window, paint/select finish, activate lamp through brief embodied cooperation with June; immediate scene persistence.
- Music: 3 easy contextual inputs in an ORIGINAL duet; missed beats never block scene.
- Time-shift traversal: 2–3 short authored geometry challenges, fast reset, no puzzle hunts.
- Finale: restore attribution (mandatory), restore verifiable content, explicitly enable NEW seasons extension; town-wide visible transformation and first snow.

## NPC companionship
June proactively initiates actions, responds to camera, proximity, speed and idle, catches up and waits. No rigid escort failure. NPC has clear authored state machine: INVITE, LEAD, WAIT, PLAY, COOPERATE, REACT, SCENE_TRANSITION. Her autonomy and career remain clear.

## Choice policy
Choices modulate tone, photo, line delivery and local staging, not historical facts or June's future. E.g. playful vs sincere compliment; '我想你留下' vs '我会想你' changes expression but cannot force June to stay. No moral score. Restoring coauthor credit is not optional.

## Guidance
No quest tabs, evidence inventory, task-card overlay or constant waypoint spam. Character voice, camera framing, environmental cues and minimal transparent context hint. If idle > reasonable threshold, June or Theo naturally guides. Never require file reading to advance.

## Failure handling
No permadeath. Fast retry after failed jump, skip repeated dialogue, autosave at scene boundaries, robust resume. Preserve old /, /p3/, /p4/ routes and saves. Bilingual ZH/EN parity required for shipped scenes.

## Measurable acceptance
Every 2–4 min one meaningful action + visible payoff; 10-min slice includes train chase, photo reaction, crooked-window collaboration and archive-driven restoration/time shift. Capture continuous Chromium run and distinguish automated pass from human enjoyment.
