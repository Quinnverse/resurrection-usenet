## Bugs found by testing and fixed

1. A ledge hung exactly one body-height over the meadow path, so a player holding "right" stopped dead under it. The scripted player in my first tests jumped over obstacles and hid this. Found when the Chinese run stalled in Chromium. Fixed with a taller hitbox, a higher jump, and ledges raised and widened. Server-room shelves got the same treatment. A regression test now walks the whole path with only "right" held.
2. The cassette never appeared on the island: the island object looked for a flag with the wrong name.
3. Mara floated 24 px above the island ground (camera and ground disagreed).
4. The picture was sized by width only, so on a 1280 × 720 desktop the Start button, the HUD and the puzzle controls fell off the screen. Now sized by window height too.
5. The island counter showed "0/0" after growth finished.
6. The camera could not scroll above the top row, cutting Mara's head off in the secret garden.
7. Interaction prompt bubbles covered the terminal screen, the cabin roof and the shed door. Each object now has its own prompt height.
8. A deploy-script check still looked for the old P3 page text.

## NOT verified, and why

- **Human play time (the 5–8 minute target): UNVERIFIED.** No person has played it. The design estimate (`P3.2-LEVEL-DESIGN.md`) is 5–8 minutes for a player who reads, explores and thinks about the puzzle. A fast player who ignores the optional content could finish in two to three minutes. The scripted minimum is about one minute.
- **How it sounds: UNVERIFIED.** The tests confirm every sound was requested, not that it is pleasant or audible. Autoplay policies, headphones and volume on real devices were not tested.
- **Whether Elena is memorable, funny and moving: UNVERIFIED.** That needs real players.
- **Browsers other than Chromium** (Firefox, Safari, Edge) and **real phones**: UNVERIFIED. The phone run is Chromium emulation with touch events.
- **Server-room floppy disks and the high shelves** are covered by unit tests of the map and by reviewed images, but **not by a browser test**.
- The **secret garden** is exercised only in the English desktop run.
- **Reduced motion**: the setting, the quick timings and the saved state are tested. How it looks with motion off was not judged beyond one frame.
- **Docker build, real daemon, real server, nginx** for the deploy package: UNVERIFIED (see `P3-TENCENT-PREVIEW.md`).
- The tool that renders scenes in Node and the scripted-play harness (`tools/`) are not a browser. All claims about real behaviour above come from CI Chromium runs.

## Known issues

- After the bridge is built, the server-room door lies under the planks and cannot be reached again. By design (it is no longer needed), but the player cannot revisit the room.
- The title screen is the live meadow, not custom title art.
- Mara's face is only a few pixels, so her expression is limited. Elena is never shown.
- No gamepad support. The phone layout is portrait. There is no landscape-specific layout.
- Saves from the earlier P3 prototype (`p3.save.v1`) are ignored (not deleted).
- `window.P3Debug.snapshot()` is exposed in the production page. It is read-only and used by the tests.
- Music is a simple generated loop and is the part most likely to need replacing.
