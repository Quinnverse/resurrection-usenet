# RESURRECTION V5 — Art, Animation and Audio Direction
Status: TARGET STYLE GUIDE, not asset inventory.

## Visual identity
Stylized painterly 3D indie adventure, cinematic silhouettes and warm lived-in environments. 1998 coastal summer: honey light, saturated sea, imperfect handmade low-poly geometry, vibrant playful energy. 2004: cleaner geometry, cooler late-day light, absences in layout and character behavior (NOT simply a grayscale filter). Present reconstruction: visible transition from archive wireframe/pixel fragments to modern coherent materials. Final winter: restrained snow and warm lighthouse beam.

## Characters
Elena: understated silhouette, thoughtful posture, dry humor, gaze that lingers; visually credible as 17 in 1998 and 23 in 2004. June: expressive posture, practical artist's clothes, camera/sketchbook, initiates play and has independent goals. Mara: modern-day 26, grounded and distinct. No visual imitation of real singers or copyrighted game characters.

## Camera and blocking
Use playable third-person for most movement; scripted short inserts only at emotionally important beats. Train: paired reverse closeups and candid camera POV; seaside cycling: wide tracking shots; crooked window: close two-shot and Elena's quiet look; 2004 cottage: held pauses and balanced dual POV, no caricatured villain angle; ending: player-controlled approach followed by broad town transformation.

## HUD and input
Near-transparent contextual interaction icon, tiny unobtrusive chapter marker if needed. No solid task cards, hard tabs, giant evidence panels, constant gold trails. Guided by companion movement, sightlines, sound and authored framing. Full ZH/EN subtitle support and adjustable contrast, reduced motion, no precision QTE requirement.

## Animation priorities
Companion WAIT/LEAD/REACT, eye contact, smile, camera shyness, reaching hand, cooperative window placement, hurt expression and silence. These must be authored believable loops; do not rely on speculative 'AI emotion' claims. First 10 minutes require 4–6 high-quality emotional animation beats rather than dozens of generic emotes.

## Audio
Entirely original music: playful train motif, quiet guitar duet, 2004 sparse reprise, final fuller winter theme. 1998 melody may be incomplete; modern orchestration is CREATED. Environmental sea/wind/train/wood/camera shutter. No use of existing non-public-domain songs, including '姐姐我等你' or '画地为牢', without licenses. Use silence intentionally in cottage confrontation.

## Asset provenance
Track creator, license, source, modifications, usage and whether historical fictional archive, reconstructed scene, or new asset. Do not commit secrets or unlicensed assets.
